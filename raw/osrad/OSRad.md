# OSRad
*A one page TTRPG of shredding, glory and eating shit in the world of Orrelia*

You're a skater in the realm of Orrelia. Elf, goblin, dwarf, orc, lowfling, whatever. You've got your board and you know the sickest spots. 

It's The Warden's Run Long Weekend, the city clears out for **3 days** while everyone watches the race in the eastern grasslands. 

You got your hands on a *Reveal Wide Mind* spell scroll, which allows you to record and create memories of your skate tricks! This is the perfect time to hit the busier spots in Glimmerhold and capture tricks for a skate video. You have 3 days to land the best tricks you can before the city returns to it's overcrowded self.

---

## YOUR SKATER

Pick a name and a species. Then assign **6 points** across four stats. No stat may exceed 3.

- **FLIP:** How many dice you roll for flip tricks. Roll FLIP+1 dice.
- **GRIND:** How many dice you roll for grinds. Roll GRIND+1 dice.
- **TECH:** How many dice you may reroll on any attempt.

Each skater has 3 **GNAR**. These are your hit points. When GNAR hits 0, you're day is over. GNAR refreshes the next day.

## SPECIES BONUSES

All folks skate, some skate different.

- **Goblin:** Start with TECH 1, GNAR 2.
- **Dwarf:** GNAR 4. FLIP and GRIND max out at 2.
- **Elf:** FLIP maxes out at 4.
- **Orc:** Slams do not tick the Kickout Clock. Still lose GNAR.
- **Lowling:** May reroll all dice holding any 4s once per spot.
- **Human:** Assign 7 points instead of 6.

## DOING TRICKS

Before rolling, **call your trick**. State whether it's a flip trick, a grind, or a combo. This determines which stat you use. Describe how you're performing the trick on the feature you're at.

**1. Roll your dice pool** (FLIP+1 or GRIND+1 d6s, or both for combos.)

**2. Check for a slam.** If two or more dice show 1 (snake eyes) at any point, you slam. Lose 1 GNAR. Tick the Kickout Clock. Your turn ends.

**3. Arrange your dice into a sequence** to build the trick. The first die in any sequence must show a 4.

**4. Reroll:** You may reroll any dice up to your TECH stat. There is no limit to how many times a single die can be rerolled. Each reroll costs 1 TECH use for the attempt.

**5. If you cannot form a valid sequence**, you bail. Tick the Kickout Clock.

**6. If you land the trick**, get some props (see PROPS).

## READING THE DICE

### The Sequence

Tricks are built by reading dice left to right. 

#### A **[4]** must always start your trick.

**[1]** is a modifier die. Placed at the start it means *switch*. Placed at the end it means *nollie*. It is not used in the middle of a sequence.

```
[ 4 ][ 2 ]        - kickflip
[ 1 ][ 4 ][ 2 ]   - switch kickflip
[ 4 ][ 2 ][ 1 ]   - nollie kickflip
```

### Flip Tricks

| Sequence | Trick |
|-------|------|
| [4] | Ollie |
| [2] | Kickflip |
| [6] | Heelflip |
| [5] | Shuvit |
| [2][3] | Tre Flip |
| [5][6] | Hardflip |
| [5][2] | FS/BS Flip |
| [3][5] | Big Spin |

Same number twice in a row doubles the board move: ```[4][2][2]``` = double kickflip, ```[4][5][5]``` = 360 shuvit.

### Grinds

| Sequence | Trick |
|-------|------|
| [6] | 50-50 |
| [2] | 5-0 |
| [3] | Boardslide |
| [5] | Lipslide |
| [6][2] | Crooked |
| [2][3] | Smith |
| [3][3] | Tailslide |
| [2][5] | Bluntslide |

Grind and flip sequences that share a notation (e.g. ```[4][2]```) are distinguished by your declared intent before the roll. "I'm going to land a doubleflip." = ```[4][2][2]``` vs "I'm going to land a kickflip 5-0." = ```[4][2]-[2]```

## CLOCKS

### The Kickout Clock

Every spot starts with a Kickout Clock of **6 ticks**. Tick it up whenever someone bails or slams. When it fills, the guards arrive, the locals shut it down, or the wizard locks the gate and you've got to find a new spot.

### The Daylight Clock

The Daylight Clock represents the day. It has **6 ticks** and does not reset between spots. Tick it at the end of each full round of skaters. When it fills, night has fallen over Orrelia and the day is over. There is no skating in the dark. Reset the clock and start the next day. You have 3 days to get as much footage as you can.

#### Lanterns

When arriving at a spot, players may tick the Kickout clock up 2 to light a lantern. Lighting a lantern removes 2 ticks from the Daylight clock.

## PROPS

You can always add a landed trick to your video part. You can only have up to 5 tricks in your part. You can remove clips for new ones.

**Clip it:** Add the trick to your Video Part. Record the spot and trick sequence to your Video Part on your character sheet.

When you land a trick you can also get some props from the homies:

**Lock it Down:** Add 1 to the spot's Legend Track with your initials. (max 6).

**Get Hype:** Restore 1 GNAR to you or a friend.

---

## THE SESSION

### Legendary Spots

Every spot has a Legend Track from 0 to 6. Track it on a d6 set aside at the spot when the players arrive.

| Legend | Effect |
|------|------|
| 1-2 | Locals are suspicious. Kickout Clock starts at 4 instead of 6. |
| 3-4 | Word is getting around. Kickout Clock starts at 6 as normal. |
| 5 | The spot is known. Skaters arrive to watch. Roll Vibe twice and pick the better result. |
| 6 | Legendary. This spot is part of Orrelia's history. You may skate it once without ticking the Kickout Clock before it resets. |

If the spot's Legend Track hits 6 while you are there, each skater may immediately attempt a trick without ticking the Daylight clock.

---

### The Video Part

At the start of the session, the party should come up with a name for their skate video.

Each skater keeps a list of their landed tricks across the session or campaign. At the end of play, the group watches the part together and decides who had the sickest tricks. That skater gets **last part**. Last part means you are the best. There is no points system, the crew just knows.

---

### S.K.8.

Two or more skaters can run a game of S.K.8. at any feature.

The first skater calls and attempts a trick. If they land it, the next skater must attempt the exact same trick. If that skater bails or cannot form the sequence, they take a letter (S, K, 8). First to spell SK8 is out.

S.K.8. does not tick the Kickout Clock or the Daylight Clock. You can Clip SK8 tricks but you don't get props for them.
